Proper nouns and product names the en-us locale would otherwise "correct" into
a different word. Each appears here in the form the consuming repo actually
writes it, including the compound identifiers typos splits into words.

Asus.ArmouryCrate is the winget package id for ASUS Armoury Crate.
The armoury entry is a package id, not British prose for an arsenal.

calibre.calibre is the winget package id for the Calibre ebook manager.
Its CLI binaries are calibre and calibredb, and CALIBRE_CONFIG_DIRECTORY
points at its profile. A step id reads calibreInstall.

Reaching for the full axe is the American spelling; en-us prefers the ax
variant and would rewrite it.
