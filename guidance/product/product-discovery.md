# Product discovery

## Interview and design

- Before proposing a solution, establish the actual problem. Before reshaping something that exists, work out what problem it solves today.

## Design and build

- When a design or format is uncertain, build several visible variations and show them side by side so the decider can pick and mix.
- Build UI prototypes on the existing app's look and structure unless a redesign is asked for. Give them live controls (sliders, pickers, toggles) that change layout, typography and color on the fly. Scale fidelity to size and scope, chosen per run or in config.

## Plan and build

- Aim early work at something small, simple, working and demonstrable rather than at design alone. When an exploration shows little value, keep the parts worth doing and drop the rest.

## Plan and review

- Close a prototype's work item only when its decisions are integrated and it looks as wanted, not when the prototype is merely built.

## Build

- Give every UI prototype an element picker, like browser devtools, so feedback can point at the exact element.
